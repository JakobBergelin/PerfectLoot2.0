What today's curves show

  • Resource decides the order more than damage does. With 5 resource a second, free skills (Frost Lance, Poison Dart, Swipe, Immolation, Trample, Sonic Ball) lead, and big spenders drop: Meteor goes from 510 to 30 on one target. Turn the limit off to see what each skill does when you can afford it.
  • Frost Lance is the strongest free attack. 50% weapon damage every 0.55 s, plus cold damage %, and it pierces a line. It beats most spenders at every investment.
  • Crit is the biggest multiplier for everyone. 62% chance at +335% damage is about ×3.1 on every skill.
  • Elemental skills gain far more from Damage %. Fire reaches ×2.9 (Damage + Elemental + Fire), physical only ×1.4. Physical weapon skills make some of it back through weapon damage (5 → 9).
  • Spells ignore weapon damage. Fireball, Meteor, Cursed Hammer and Cold Snap use flat numbers only.
  • Health and armor skills grow the most. Immolation and Trample scale with health (10 → 136, ×13.6), Sonic Ball with armor. They start as the weakest and end far ahead.
  • Cooldown skills (Cold Snap, Riposte, Phantom Edge) stay small next to repeated attacks at every investment.

Assumptions

  • Fresh character: weapon damage 5, no affixes. Maxed: the highest total each stat can reach on gear (one stat at a time, so not one real set of items).
  • A pack is 5 enemies within 3 m. A skill hits all of them once its radius covers 3 m.
  • Hit counts I had to guess: Multishot hits one target with 2 arrows, Flamethrower keeps about 5 flames on a target, Frozen Orb hits with 4 shards, a Poison Dart explosion every 4 s, Tesla Coil channels 3 s per use, Trample is held 80% of the time.
  • Barrage turns resource spent on other skills into missiles; it uses the income (10/s without the limit).
  • Resource investment adds up to +11.8 regeneration a second to the income slider and makes skills up to 52% cheaper. Resource on hit is not modeled; it would favour skills that hit many enemies fast.
  • Captain's Shield in Proposed: a pack takes 3 hits, plus one per bounce from gear (up to 3 more).
  • Unique: pierce goes up to 7 and each pierce adds 20% more shard hits in a pack. Projectile speed goes up to +75%, and the hammer gains 60% of it as damage. Frozen enemies go up to 50% of the time. Whirlwind's ramp counts as reached when your resource covers its cost. Multishot: about 3 arrows hit one enemy, all 6 land in a pack.
  • Not modeled: companions (Hydra, Wolf, Stone Guardian, Thralls, Mirror Image, Wisp), buffs (Frenzy, Blood Rite, Stances, Shadowstep), defenses and movement skills, enemy armor and resistances.
